# Acme Group — Programme Manager, Supply Chain Transformation (synthetic example JD)

Source: example. Location: Amsterdam (hybrid, 2 days). Salary stated: EUR 80–95k base + 10% bonus.

## About the role
Acme Group operates 40 distribution centres across Europe. We are replacing our warehouse management system and automating three flagship sites over the next 30 months. The Programme Manager owns delivery across internal teams and four vendors, reports to the VP Supply Chain Transformation, and chairs the monthly steering committee.

## What you will do
- Own the integrated programme plan, budget (EUR 15M) and benefits case
- Run governance: steering committee, risk and dependency management, stage gates
- Manage vendor delivery and commercial performance against SLAs
- Lead a team of three project managers and a PMO analyst
- Land change in the operations organisation: training, cut-over, hypercare

## What we look for
- 7+ years of programme or project management in logistics, manufacturing or retail operations
- Delivery of at least one WMS or automation programme end to end
- Experience recovering troubled programmes
- Vendor and contract management
- PRINCE2, MSP or PMP; SAFe a plus
- Fluent English; Dutch or German helpful
- Right to work in the EU
